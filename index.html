Haider Auto Poster

Haider Auto Poster is a small publishing tool that helps a content creator share his own new short videos to his authorized TikTok account automatically, shortly after they are published.

How it works

1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and approves access.
2. When the creator publishes a new video, the tool uploads it to the connected TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API.
3. The account owner reviews and controls every post, including caption and privacy settings.

Who it is for

This tool is used only by the creator and the people he has authorized to manage his content. It does not post content belonging to anyone else.
