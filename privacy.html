Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you connect TikTok, we receive an access token, a refresh token, your TikTok open ID and basic profile information (display name and avatar), as allowed by the permissions you approve. We also handle the video files and captions you choose to post.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to upload and publish videos to your connected TikTok account and to show you the status of your posts.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, through its official API, to complete your posts.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored on a private, secured server and are used only by the Service. Video files are deleted from our server after they are posted.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect the Service at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). To have your stored data deleted, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
