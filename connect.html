Connect your TikTok account

Step 1. Click the button and sign in to TikTok. Approve these permissions:

  • user.info.basic – show your display name and avatar so you can confirm the right account
  • video.upload – send your new videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts
  • video.publish – post your new videos to your profile with the settings you choose

Step 2. After approving, you'll see a connection code. The Haider Auto Poster server uses it to finish connecting your account.

Step 3. When the creator publishes a new video, it's sent to your TikTok account automatically. You can disconnect at any time in TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
